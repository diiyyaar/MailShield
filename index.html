About MailShield

MailShield is a beginner-friendly cybersecurity project developed to help users recognize phishing emails. It analyzes email content for suspicious keywords such as "urgent", "verify", "password", and "click here", then provides a risk level and safety recommendations. This project aims to spread cybersecurity awareness and help users stay safe from phishing attacks.
